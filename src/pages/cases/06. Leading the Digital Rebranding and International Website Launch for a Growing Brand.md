## Project Status

**Ongoing Project**

## Challenge

The company initiated a major rebranding effort to support expansion into international regions.

The existing website no longer reflected the future positioning of the brand, and there was no established roadmap for how the digital platform should support the company's growth strategy.

The new website needed to support:
- Four language versions
- Six international regions
- Approximately 1,200 pages
- Different product portfolios across markets

Website ownership across all corporate websites was managed by a team of three, including myself as Product Owner.

The challenge was not only to redesign a website, but to create a scalable digital platform capable of supporting an evolving international business.

## My Role

As the Product Owner for the initiative, I am responsible for defining requirements, managing stakeholders, coordinating external partners, and driving execution.

Because the company has no dedicated UX or design specialists, I also define information architecture, create wireframes, design page structures, and translate business requirements into user experiences.

Development is delivered by a separate engineering organization. I work closely with the Engineering Manager and Project Manager to align priorities, requirements, and delivery plans.

## Discovery and Strategic Planning

The project began with a discovery phase aimed at defining the future role of the website within the company's rebranding strategy.

I led:
- A full website audit
- Competitor analysis
- Assessment of strengths and weaknesses
- Definition of website vision and positioning
- Development of a digital strategy supporting international growth

The objective was to establish a clear strategic direction before committing to design and implementation decisions.

## Managing Design Exploration

To explore possible creative directions, two external agencies were commissioned to develop six design concepts.

Customer research conducted by the analytics team gathered feedback from existing customers and helped stakeholders evaluate the alternatives.

Following concept selection, I became the primary point of contact between the business and the selected agency, ensuring that business objectives were translated into actionable design requirements.

## Balancing Design Quality and Budget

One of the key decisions involved optimizing the use of external design resources.

Rather than outsourcing the entire website design, I proposed focusing agency efforts on the most complex and strategically important pages.

This approach allowed us to:
- Apply specialist expertise where it delivered the greatest value
- Reduce overall design costs
- Accelerate production of supporting pages

Using the approved design principles and component library, I create wireframes and page layouts internally for the remaining sections of the website.

## Aligning Stakeholders Across Markets

A significant portion of the project involves gathering and reconciling requirements from multiple stakeholders.

Key decisions include
- Regional launch priorities
- Language strategy
- Market-specific product visibility
- Content ownership and governance

I facilitate ongoing workshops and planning sessions to transform business discussions into clear implementation requirements.

## Driving Parallel Delivery

To reduce delivery time, I established a workflow where planning, design, content preparation, and development activities progress in parallel.

My responsibilities include:
- Maintaining the product roadmap
- Defining and prioritizing requirements
- Creating wireframes and page concepts
- Preparing development tasks
- Coordinating external agencies
- Managing dependencies across teams
- Supporting stakeholder decision-making

This allows the project to move forward while strategic, design, and technical decisions continue to evolve.

## Current Status

The project is currently in the early implementation phase.

The strategic direction has been defined, design concepts have been validated, development activities have started, and market-specific requirements continue to be refined through stakeholder collaboration.

## Key Takeaway

This project is not simply a website redesign.

It is a digital transformation initiative supporting a broader rebranding and international expansion strategy.

My role is to establish product direction, align stakeholders, manage external partners, optimize resource allocation, and convert business objectives into a practical delivery roadmap for a multilingual, multi-region digital platform.
